Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

Is UtilityHub completely free?
Yes, UtilityHub is completely free to use. There are no premium tiers, subscriptions, or hidden fees. We believe essential tools should be accessible to everyone.
How is my data stored?
Your privacy is our priority. All data (notes, expenses, todos, passwords) is saved locally in your browser's LocalStorage. We do not use external databases or track your inputs.
Can I use it offline?
Absolutely. Because UtilityHub is built as a self-contained single file architecture, once it's loaded in your browser, it requires zero internet connection to function.
Will more utilities be added in the future?
The current architecture is highly scalable. Future updates can seamlessly introduce new tools directly into the dashboard while maintaining the single-file constraint.

About UtilityHub

The premium, open-source, client-side utility suite.

Our Philosophy

We built UtilityHub because we were tired of encountering ad-ridden, slow, and privacy-invasive web tools for simple tasks. Generating a password, calculating BMI, or converting units shouldn't require sending data to a remote server.

UtilityHub is built on a single-file architecture. This means the entire application—every CSS style, logic script, and HTML node—lives inside one file. Once loaded, it never makes a network request again.

Architecture & Technology

UtilityHub is proudly built without heavy frameworks.

  • Pure HTML5: Semantic and accessible structuring.
  • Vanilla CSS3: Custom property themes, CSS grid, flexbox, and glassmorphism.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Modular class-based logic, strict event delegation, and zero dependencies.
  • Local Storage API: Persistent data handling right in your browser.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Complete Local Execution

UtilityHub operates strictly on the client-side. This means that 100% of the computations, data processing, and data storage occur locally on your device within your web browser.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data. We do not use analytics trackers, crash reporters, or third-party advertising scripts.

3. LocalStorage Usage

The application uses your browser's native LocalStorage API to save your preferences and utility data (such as Notes, Todos, Expenses, and Calculation History). This data is completely isolated and cannot be accessed by external servers.

4. Clearing Your Data

You have full control over your data. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or by using the specific "Clear History" buttons provided within the individual utilities.
